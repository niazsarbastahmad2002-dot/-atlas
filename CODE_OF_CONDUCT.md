# Code of Conduct

Atlas aims to maintain a professional, useful, and safe open-source community.

## Expected behavior

Participants should:

- communicate respectfully and focus criticism on ideas, code, and evidence
- make space for people with different levels of experience
- give actionable technical feedback
- protect private, patient, and security-sensitive information
- disclose relevant limitations or uncertainty rather than overstating results
- prioritize the safety and reliability of clinic-facing workflows

## Unacceptable behavior

The following are not acceptable:

- harassment, threats, intimidation, or personal attacks
- discriminatory or demeaning language
- publishing another person's private information without permission
- intentionally exposing credentials, patient information, or security-sensitive material
- knowingly misrepresenting test results, security findings, adoption, or project activity
- using project spaces primarily for spam or unrelated promotion

## Scope

This code applies to Atlas repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other project-controlled community spaces.

## Reporting concerns

For ordinary community concerns, contact the project maintainer privately through the contact method available on the maintainer's GitHub profile.

For suspected security vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead of opening a public issue.

Reports should contain only the information necessary to investigate the concern. Do not include patient data, production credentials, access tokens, or other sensitive information.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove inappropriate content, close or lock discussions, reject contributions, or restrict participation when necessary to protect the project and its community.

Enforcement should be proportionate, documented when appropriate, and focused on restoring a safe and productive project environment.
